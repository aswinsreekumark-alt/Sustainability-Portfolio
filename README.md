## Projects

### 1. Digital Product Carbon Footprint & BOM-to-PCF Automation

An illustrative cradle-to-gate carbon footprint assessment of a 5.5 kW industrial three-phase induction motor.

**Tools:** Python, Pandas, SQLite, Excel, openLCA, ecoinvent 3.9.1, Power BI

**Key work:**
- Cleaned and validated a simulated 10-component engineering Bill of Materials (BOM).
- Developed a structured BOM-to-environmental-dataset mapping.
- Modelled material supply and an illustrative manufacturing electricity input in openLCA.
- Assessed climate change impacts using IPCC 2021 GWP100.
- Performed electricity-consumption sensitivity analysis.
- Developed a Power BI dashboard to communicate results.

**Preliminary modelled result:** 174.93 kg CO₂-eq per motor, including material supply and an illustrative 10 kWh electricity assumption.

*Note: This is a demonstration project using simulated BOM data and illustrative manufacturing assumptions, not a manufacturer-verified product footprint.*
